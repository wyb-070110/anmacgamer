The mirror window displays your phone screen on your Mac and forwards your mouse, keyboard, and gamepad input to the phone.

![The mirror window](figure:mirror-overview)

## Direct mouse & keyboard input

When key mapping is off, the window acts like a touch screen:

- Click to tap, drag to swipe, and scroll with the mouse wheel.
- Typing sends text directly to the phone.

When key mapping is on, your active profile's keys control the game instead. See [Profiles and sharing](help:profiles) and [Key mapping editor](help:keymap-editor).

## Toolbar & customization

**Back**, **Home**, and **Recents** are fixed on the toolbar, plus up to **5 buttons** you choose (**More > Toolbar buttons…**), including **Keep on top**. Change when the toolbar appears in **Settings > Mirroring & quality > Display > Toolbar** (pinned, hover at top, or shortcut only).

![The toolbar](figure:mirror-toolbar)

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| :keyboard: Key mapping & options | Toggle key mapping, adjust key hint opacity, switch profiles, or open the editor. |
| :house: Back, Home, Recents | Phone navigation buttons. |
| :rotate-cw: Orientation | Follow device rotation, or lock Portrait / Landscape. |
| :power: Power | Turn off only the phone's physical screen while mirroring continues. |
| :volume-2: Volume | Mute or adjust phone sound on your Mac. The phone's physical volume keys do not change it while mirroring. |
| :bot: Tasks | Start or stop automation [tasks](help:scripts) on this phone. |
| Record | Record video, audio, and inputs. View clips under **More > Recordings…**. |
| :puzzle: Extension panels | Open side panels or buttons added by extensions. |
| :activity: Diagnostics | Show live FPS, latency, and decoder stats. |
| :maximize-2: Full screen | Fill the screen (<kbd>F11</kbd> to enter or exit). |
| :ellipsis: More | Status capsule mode, **Recordings…**, **Edit logic**, **Vision workbench**, **Measure the real latency**, **Mini window**, **Keep on top**, **Device settings**, and **Disconnect**. |
| :pin: Pin | Keep the toolbar visible above the picture. |

## Status capsule & key hints

- **Status capsule**: shows FPS, latency, and connection type in the bottom-right corner. Click to toggle diagnostics; right-click to set **Automatic**, **Always shown**, or **Hidden**.
- **Key hints**: show mapped keys on screen when a profile loads, then fade. Adjust opacity in **Key mapping options** or **Settings > Input & key mapping**.

## Lock the camera

In shooters and action games with a **View** component, press the camera lock key (above Tab, left of 1, or as set in the profile) to hide the pointer and turn the camera with mouse movement. Press it again to release the pointer.

![Lock the camera](figure:camera-lock)

Turn off the edge glow under **Settings > Mirroring & quality > Display > Glow while the camera is locked**.

## Type into the phone

With [anmacgamer Companion](help:companion) installed, opening a text box on the phone switches the window to typing mode automatically: press **Enter** to send or **Esc** to return to the game.

## Full screen, mini window & latency test

![Full screen and the mini window](figure:window-modes)

- **Mini window**: choose **More > Shrink to a mini window on top** for a small always-on-top view. Drag to move; double-click or click **Restore** to expand.
- **Measure real latency**: choose **More > Measure the real latency**, then tap or press keys over darker areas of the screen to measure end-to-end touch-to-display delay.
- **Apps**: on the **Devices** page, use **More > Apps** on a mirrored phone to launch apps on the phone or an independent display.

Closing the mirror window disconnects the phone. If you close while editing keys, choose **Leave editing** (saves and keeps mirroring) or **Close the mirror window**.
